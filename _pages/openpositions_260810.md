This page contains information as of August 10, 2026.

**Joining the group as a postdoc**

Applications for postdoctoral positions are accepted on a rolling basis. We are seeking candidates with a PhD in Statistics, Biostatistics, Quantitative Psychology, or related areas. Interested candidates should email their CV, a one-page statement of research interests, and contact information for two referees.

​**Joining the group as a PhD student**

We are seeking (up to) two PhD students to join our group in Fall 2027, either in Statistics or Psychology. Interested applicants should apply directly to the University of Toronto [School of Graduate Studies Online Application Portal](https://admissions.sgs.utoronto.ca/apply/) to be formally considered and indicate me (Jun Young Park) as a potential supervisor. please note that applications for PhD programs at the University of Toronto are typically due in late November.

We are seeking applicants with strong quantitative backgrounds, especially in mathematics and statistics, and expertise in computer science is also a plus. Our group has been developing novel statistical methods for advancing science. Please visit [our research page](https://junjypark.github.io/research/) to see if our group aligns well with your research interests. Of course, proposing new research directions would be highly valued and welcome. 

You may also send your CV/resume, transcript, and a one-page statement of research interests to discuss your fit to our group. Please note that, due to the high volume of emails received, there might be some delays in responses, and your patience would be greatly appreciated.

​**Joining the group as a master's or undergraduate student**

As of August 10, 2026, there are currently no reading courses or research assistantships available in 2026. However, if the situation changes, the information will be shared here, and relevant updates will be communicated through the departmental channel.
